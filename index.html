Why I started it

For the past two years, I've had the incredible opportunity of volunteering for Operation Smile during their medical missions in Monterrey, Mexico—translating between surgeons and nurses and playing with child patients before life-changing cleft palate surgeries.

As ASFM OpSmile Liaison, still in high school, I was given the chance to make an actual difference. This experience convinced me: young people like myself can change the world. That's exactly why, in March of 2025, I founded the UNbound Youth Initiative... and why, only a few months later, my team and I united schools across the globe around this shared belief.

EDES 120/220 · Camp For All

Parity Aim.

An adaptive stand and trigger so campers with limited hand or arm function can play paintball on their own.

Mission

Improve access to paintball for Camp For All campers with limited hand or arm function, so they can play more independently.

Design Analysis →

Stage 02

Design Analysis

Some campers can aim but can't hold the marker or pull the trigger. Existing adaptive systems cost up to $3,000.

Design criteria

  • SafeNXL guidelines
  • Stable≥ 100 N
  • Sturdy trigger≥ 10 N
  • Easy to use · #1 objectiveLevel 4 / 5 independence
  • Enduring≥ 10,400 uses
  • Economical≤ $40
  • Easy to maintain< 20 s to detach

First three are constraints; the rest are ranked objectives.
